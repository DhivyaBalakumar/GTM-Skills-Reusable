# GTM rep interview guide (30 min)

**Role:** BDR / AE / Manager · **Name:** · **Tenure:** · **Date:**

## Warm-up (3 min)
- Walk me through yesterday from first login to last task.

## Data (7 min)
- When you pick an account to work, where do you look first? Second?
- Last time the CRM was wrong or missing something — what happened? What did you do?
- Where do you keep your own list/spreadsheet? Why not in the CRM?

## Outreach & pipeline (7 min)
- How do you decide who to contact today?
- Show me the last sequence/email you sent. How long did research take?
- When an inbound lead comes in, how do you find out? How fast do you reach out?

## AE work (7 min, AEs)
- After a call, how long until the CRM is updated? Who does it?
- How long does a proposal take, start to sent? Which part is slowest?

## Wrap-up (6 min)
- If I could take one task off your plate tomorrow, which one?
- What tool would you happily never open again?
- Anything we tried before that failed? Why?
